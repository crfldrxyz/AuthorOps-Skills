# Domain Map

The taxonomy is intentionally functional rather than tied to a single user persona.

- `writing`: create and shape ideas, arguments, introductions, conclusions, and transitions.
- `editing`: improve or audit language at sentence and paragraph level.
- `transformation`: change form, length, complexity, or voice while preserving meaning.
- `research`: questions, sources, evidence, synthesis, methods, and bias.
- `citations`: citation generation, style, completeness, mapping, and references.
- `academic`: paper-specific structure and scholarly conventions.
- `manuscripts`: long-form document architecture, consistency, and readiness.
- `books`: book and narrative architecture.
- `publishing`: venue and submission preparation.
- `quality`: reasoning and content quality controls.
- `forensics`: provenance, attribution, quotation, similarity risk, and final audit.

A skill may be related to other domains without being duplicated across them.
