# Evidence Policy

AuthorOps-Skills separates linguistic judgment from external verification.

## Evidence classes
- **Text evidence:** directly observable in the supplied document.
- **Source evidence:** information retrieved from a cited or supplied source.
- **Corpus evidence:** similarity or frequency evidence from an available corpus/tool.
- **Tool evidence:** a result returned by an explicitly available external service.

A skill must not imply an evidence class it did not actually access.

## High-risk claims
Plagiarism, factual correctness, source authority, citation correctness, legal/ethical compliance, and statistical validity require evidence beyond stylistic intuition. When that evidence is unavailable, flag the limitation instead of inventing certainty.
