# Academic Paper Structure

Identify the paper's research question, contribution, argument, evidence, method, results, interpretation, and conclusion. Evaluate whether the document structure makes those relationships visible.

Do not assume one universal IMRaD structure applies to every discipline. Use the declared genre and target venue when available.

Return structural map, gaps, ordering issues, and prioritized recommendations.
