# Results-Discussion Alignment

Match discussion statements to reported findings. Flag new numbers, unsupported causal language, generalization beyond the sample, and interpretations that contradict the results. Preserve legitimate theoretical interpretation when explicitly justified.

Quality gates: evidence traceability and calibrated claims.
