# Abstract Check

Compare the abstract against the manuscript. Check factual alignment, completeness for the target genre, specificity, economy, and whether claims in the abstract are stronger than the paper supports.

Report omissions, mismatches, unsupported claims, and optional improvements. Never invent results or contributions.
