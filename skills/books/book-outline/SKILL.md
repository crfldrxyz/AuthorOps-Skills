# Book Outline

Define the book's central promise; assign each chapter a distinct contribution; test progression, balance, repetition, and reader payoff. For fiction, track narrative movement; for nonfiction, track conceptual accumulation.

Quality gates: every chapter earns its place and the sequence advances the book's promise.
