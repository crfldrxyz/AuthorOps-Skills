# Pacing Check

Track scene or section length, escalation, reflection, exposition, repetition, and payoff relative to the work's genre and intended effect. Flag prolonged stagnation or rushed major turns.

Quality gates: interpret pacing relative to purpose and genre; do not reduce it to page counts alone.
