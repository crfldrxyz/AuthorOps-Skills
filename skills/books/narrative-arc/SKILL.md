# Narrative Arc

Track inciting movement, escalation, turning points, reversals, consequences, climax or culmination, and resolution. For nonlinear structures, assess causal or thematic legibility rather than assuming chronological order.

Quality gates: rely on the supplied story, not invented authorial intent.
