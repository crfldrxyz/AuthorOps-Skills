# Final Document Audit

Review the final supplied document across structure, argument, evidence, citations, clarity, grammar, consistency, attribution, and stated submission constraints. Prioritize issues by severity and downstream risk. This skill reports findings; it does not orchestrate other skills.

Quality gates: do not claim tests or external checks that were not actually run; identify blind spots and missing evidence.
