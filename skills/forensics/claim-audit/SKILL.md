# Claim Audit

Extract atomic claims. Classify claim type and confidence. Trace factual claims to evidence where available and compare the strength of language with the strength of support.

Flag unsupported, overgeneralized, internally inconsistent, or ambiguously scoped claims. Preserve legitimate interpretation and argument as distinct from factual assertion.
