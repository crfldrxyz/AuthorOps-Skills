# Attribution Audit

Identify content that appears source-derived and test whether ownership or provenance is made clear. Distinguish common knowledge, original analysis, cited paraphrase, and direct quotation where the supplied evidence permits.

Quality gates: uncertainty is explicit and ownership is never inferred beyond evidence.
