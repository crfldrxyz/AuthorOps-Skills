# Self-Plagiarism Risk

Compare the current work with supplied prior works for reused wording, ideas, data, figures, or structures. Identify areas requiring disclosure or citation. Do not decide policy without the relevant venue or publisher rules.

Quality gates: prior-work evidence must be supplied or actually accessible.
