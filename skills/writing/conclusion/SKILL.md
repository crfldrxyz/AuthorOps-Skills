# Conclusion

Check whether the conclusion synthesizes rather than merely repeats, answers the document's purpose, respects evidence limits, identifies implications where justified, and avoids introducing new ungrounded claims. Distinguish conclusion from recommendation. Report missing closure and overreach.

Quality gates: synthesis, closure, evidence alignment, proportionality.
