# Transitions

Identify the relationship between adjacent units: continuation, contrast, cause, example, qualification, sequence, or shift. Flag abrupt movement or redundant transition markers. Prefer conceptual continuity over decorative connectors. When rewriting, preserve the actual logical relation.

Quality gates: truthful relationship, local clarity, non-redundancy, and natural flow.
