# Outline Design

## Purpose
Construct a document architecture that makes the intended reasoning or narrative visible.

## Procedure
Identify the document's primary objective; define section purposes; order sections by logical or narrative dependency; assign each section a job; expose missing transitions and unsupported leaps.

## Inputs
Topic, purpose, audience, source material, constraints, and target format.

## Constraints
Do not add unsupported claims merely to fill an outline. Avoid generic headings whose purpose is unclear.

## Output
Provide hierarchy, section purpose, key content, dependency notes, and unresolved gaps.

## Quality gates
Every major section has one dominant job; sequence is defensible; no required argument step is silently missing.
