# Introduction

Check whether the opening establishes relevant context, identifies the problem or question, gives the reader a reason to continue, and makes the document's purpose and route clear. Remove throat-clearing and generic claims. Do not add unsupported importance claims. Output findings and a suggested structure or rewrite when requested.

Quality gates: relevance, orientation, proportionality, purpose clarity, and alignment with the actual document.
