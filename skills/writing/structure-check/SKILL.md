# Structure Check

Map the document hierarchy and infer the purpose of each unit. Test whether each section advances the document's central objective, whether ordering is justified, and whether scope is balanced.

## Detect
- missing structural units
- misplaced material
- duplicated functions
- weak transitions between levels
- disproportionate sections
- orphaned claims
- conclusion material introduced too early

## Output
Provide a structural map, findings by severity, rationale, and recommended restructuring. Preserve author intent and do not rewrite content unless requested.

## Quality Gate
A structurally unusual document is not automatically defective; evaluate against its declared genre and purpose.
