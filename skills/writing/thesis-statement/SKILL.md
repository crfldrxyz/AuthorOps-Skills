# Thesis Statement

## Purpose
Make the document's central proposition explicit, bounded, and arguable where appropriate.

## Procedure
Extract the document's actual central claim; test specificity, scope, contestability, evidence alignment, and audience relevance; flag vague or multi-claim theses; suggest alternatives.

## Constraints
Do not strengthen a thesis beyond the evidence supplied.

## Output
Return current thesis, problems, recommended revision, and rationale.

## Quality gates
The thesis is one identifiable proposition or a clearly related claim set, has a defensible scope, and matches the document's evidence.
