# Idea Generation

## Purpose
Turn a goal, problem, audience, or rough premise into a useful set of distinct writing directions.

## When to use
Use before outlining when the concept is ambiguous, underdeveloped, or has several viable angles.

## Inputs
Topic, audience, purpose, constraints, existing notes, and desired form.

## Procedure
1. Identify the central problem or opportunity.
2. Extract explicit constraints and implied assumptions.
3. Generate distinct concepts rather than cosmetic variants.
4. Rank by usefulness, originality, feasibility, and fit.
5. State unresolved assumptions.

## Constraints
Do not fabricate evidence or present speculation as research findings.

## Output contract
Return ideas with premise, value, risk, and next-step recommendation.

## Quality gates
Ideas must be meaningfully distinct, scoped, and aligned to the stated audience and purpose.
