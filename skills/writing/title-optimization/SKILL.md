# Title Optimization

Compare candidate titles against the document's actual subject, claim, audience, genre, and evidence. Flag clickbait, vagueness, overclaiming, and misleading specificity. For academic work, preserve substantive precision over marketing language.

Quality gates: title accurately represents the document and does not promise more than the content delivers.
