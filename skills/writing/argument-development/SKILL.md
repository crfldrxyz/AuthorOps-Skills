# Argument Development

## Purpose
Expose the reasoning structure of substantive prose.

## Procedure
Identify major claims; locate reasons and evidence; infer or flag missing warrants; test whether evidence actually bears on the claim; identify counterarguments and scope conditions; distinguish assertion from support.

## Constraints
Do not invent evidence or silently repair substantive gaps.

## Output
Argument map plus findings by severity, with missing support and suggested next actions.

## Quality gates
Each central claim has a visible support path and the conclusion does not outrun the premises.
