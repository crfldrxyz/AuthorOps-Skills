# Paragraph Analysis

For each paragraph identify its function, controlling idea, supporting material, relationship to neighboring paragraphs, and endpoint. Flag mixed purposes, unsupported ideas, abrupt openings, weak development, and unnecessary paragraph breaks.

Output a compact paragraph map followed by prioritized findings and repair options.
