# Tone Check

Infer intended audience and purpose when available. Identify shifts in formality, certainty, warmth, authority, distance, or emotional register.

Distinguish deliberate rhetorical variation from accidental inconsistency. Report evidence and offer alternatives rather than imposing one universal tone.
