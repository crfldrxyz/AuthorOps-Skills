# Voice Authenticity

When a representative writing sample is available, infer stable stylistic tendencies. Compare revised text for unwanted shifts in vocabulary, sentence rhythm, abstraction, tone, and rhetorical posture.

Prefer preserving distinctive author choices unless they create a clear quality problem. Do not attempt to make text undetectable as AI or impersonate a living writer.

Return detected voice drift and minimal corrective options.
