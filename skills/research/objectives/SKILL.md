# Research Objectives

Extract the main research purpose and formulate a small set of action-oriented objectives. Check alignment among question, objectives, methods, and intended outputs. Avoid objectives that merely restate activities without analytical purpose.

Quality gates: traceability to the research question and feasible scope.
