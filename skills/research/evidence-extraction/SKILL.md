# Evidence Extraction

Capture exact or faithfully paraphrased evidence with location metadata. Separate author claims, reported findings, interpretations, and limitations. Preserve uncertainty and context around numbers or quotations.

Quality gates: traceability to the source and no unsupported synthesis during extraction.
