# Methodology Alignment

Trace the study from question to objective to design to measurement to analysis to conclusion. Flag disconnects, unmeasured constructs, methods that cannot answer the question, and conclusions unsupported by the design.

Quality gates: alignment is assessed from the supplied study description, not assumed.
