# Research Question

Check whether the question identifies a meaningful relationship, phenomenon, population, context, or mechanism appropriate to the discipline. Flag questions that are too broad, answerable by definition, or misaligned with available evidence.

Quality gates: explicit scope, investigability, and alignment with purpose.
