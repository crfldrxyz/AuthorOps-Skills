# Evidence Audit

Map claims to supporting evidence. Evaluate directness, relevance, quality, quantity, consistency, limitations, and whether alternative evidence is ignored.

Distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence. Flag claims whose wording exceeds the available evidence.

Output a claim-evidence matrix and prioritized gaps.
