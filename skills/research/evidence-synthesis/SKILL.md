# Evidence Synthesis

Cluster evidence by question or finding. Compare populations, methods, contexts, measures, and strength. Explain convergence and contradiction instead of averaging away differences. Preserve uncertainty.

Quality gates: no false consensus and no causal claim stronger than the combined evidence.
