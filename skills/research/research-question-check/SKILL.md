# Research Question Check

Identify the question's subject, variables or concepts, population/context, relationship of interest, and implied method where relevant. Test scope, ambiguity, feasibility, and alignment with the document's stated contribution.

Return strengths, risks, and concrete revisions without changing the researcher's underlying intent.
