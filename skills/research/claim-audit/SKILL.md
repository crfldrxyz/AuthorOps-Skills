# Claim Audit

List material claims and inspect their support. Flag unsupported assertions, scope drift, missing qualification, mismatched citations, and claims that require external verification. Distinguish factual, interpretive, normative, and predictive claims.

Quality gates: claim-specific findings and honest verification limits.
