# Literature Review Analysis

Separate summary from synthesis. Identify themes, debates, methodological patterns, evidence gaps, contradictions, and the review's connection to the research problem.

Flag source dumping, unsupported gap claims, outdated framing when currency matters, and conclusions that do not follow from the reviewed literature.

Return a synthesis map and prioritized gaps.
