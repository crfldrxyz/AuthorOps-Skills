# Literature Review

Group sources by concepts, methods, findings, or debates rather than author-by-author summary when appropriate. Identify synthesis, contradictions, methodological differences, and missing perspectives. Separate evidence from interpretation.

Quality gates: traceable claims, balanced coverage, and a clear link to the research question.
