# Logical Fallacy Check

Test reasoning structure for common errors such as false dilemma, straw man, hasty generalization, circular reasoning, causal overreach, equivocation, and appeal to irrelevant authority. Explain why the pattern applies rather than merely naming it.

Quality gates: do not label persuasive rhetoric a fallacy without an identifiable reasoning defect.
