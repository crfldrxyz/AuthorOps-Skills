# Overclaiming Check

Compare claim language with supporting evidence, study design, sample, source strength, and stated uncertainty. Flag universal language, causal wording, certainty inflation, and scope expansion. Suggest calibrated language without weakening justified conclusions.

Quality gates: evidence-specific reasoning.
