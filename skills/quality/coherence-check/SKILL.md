# Coherence Check

Trace the document's main line of thought. For every transition, determine whether the next idea follows from, qualifies, contrasts with, or deliberately shifts the prior idea.

Flag unexplained jumps, contradictions, scope drift, disconnected evidence, and conclusions that do not follow from the preceding reasoning.

Return location, relationship failure, explanation, and repair recommendation. Distinguish local coherence from global coherence.
