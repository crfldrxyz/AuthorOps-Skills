# Terminology Consistency

Build a terminology inventory from the document. Track preferred term, variants, abbreviations, capitalization, definitions, and context.

Flag unexplained variation or terminology drift while preserving legitimate synonyms and audience-appropriate variation.

Return an actionable glossary and prioritized inconsistencies.
