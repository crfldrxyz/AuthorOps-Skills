# Redundancy Check

Compare propositions across the document, not merely identical wording. Distinguish useful emphasis from information duplication. Identify repeated claims, examples, definitions, and evidence.

Return the locations involved, relationship, severity, and whether to delete, merge, relocate, or retain.
