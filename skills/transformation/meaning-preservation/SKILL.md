# Meaning Preservation

Compare source and target propositions dimension by dimension. Flag strengthened or weakened certainty, changed causal relations, lost limitations, shifted scope, altered attribution, or newly introduced information.

Quality gates: every finding is grounded in a specific semantic difference.
