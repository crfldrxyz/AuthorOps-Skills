# Paraphrase

First identify the source proposition and its qualifiers. Rewrite at the conceptual level rather than replacing words mechanically. Preserve attribution, uncertainty, causal relationships, quantities, scope, and technical terms.

Return the paraphrase and a meaning-preservation check. Never present a paraphrase as original research or remove the source citation.

## Quality Gate
A successful paraphrase must remain semantically faithful while being independently worded and structurally distinct.
