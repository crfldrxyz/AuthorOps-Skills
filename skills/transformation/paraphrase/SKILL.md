# Paraphrase

Understand the source proposition before rewriting. Change syntax and wording at the conceptual level rather than substituting synonyms. Preserve claims, qualifiers, quantities, causal direction, and attribution. Keep source citation requirements intact.

Quality gates: meaning preservation, source attribution, no invented content, and substantive distance from the source wording.
