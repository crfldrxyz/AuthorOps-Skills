# Voice Authenticity

Compare vocabulary, sentence rhythm, abstraction, rhetorical habits, transitions, tone, and degree of formality against supplied author samples. Flag likely inconsistencies; do not claim authorship detection or detector evasion.

Quality gates: require reference samples for comparative claims and distinguish preference from measurable pattern.
