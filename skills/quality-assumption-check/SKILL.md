# Assumption Check

Surface explicit and necessary implicit assumptions. Classify them as empirical, methodological, definitional, normative, or practical. Flag assumptions that are unsupported, hidden, or contradicted by the supplied evidence.

Quality gates: distinguish necessary inference from speculative mind-reading.
