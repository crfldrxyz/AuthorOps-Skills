# Clarity Check

Inspect ambiguous pronouns, overloaded sentences, undefined terms, hidden actors, weak referents, excessive abstraction, unclear causal relations, and missing local context. Explain why a reader may struggle and suggest targeted fixes.

Quality gates: identify the actual source of confusion; do not simplify away necessary precision.
