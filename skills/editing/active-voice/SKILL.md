# Active Voice

Find passive constructions, identify the grammatical or conceptual actor, and distinguish useful passive voice from avoidable opacity. Suggest active rewrites only when the actor is known or appropriately inferable.

Quality gates: never invent an actor and never force active voice when focus or scientific convention favors passive construction.
