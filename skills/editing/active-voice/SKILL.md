# Active Voice

Identify passive constructions, recover the actor when the source makes it knowable, and suggest active alternatives only when they improve the passage.

Do not mechanically convert passive voice. Passive voice is valid when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, obvious, or when the affected entity deserves emphasis.

Report original, diagnosis, optional active revision, and confidence.
