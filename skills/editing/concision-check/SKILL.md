# Concision Check

Identify filler, repeated propositions, inflated constructions, redundant modifiers, and unnecessary nominalizations. Verify every cut preserves meaning, qualification, evidence, and voice.

Prefer deletion before substitution. Return prioritized findings with location, rationale, proposed edit, and confidence.

## Quality Gate
Never shorten text by removing necessary nuance or technical precision.
