# Concision Check

Find filler, needless repetition, inflated nominalizations, duplicated ideas, and long constructions that can be compressed safely. Preserve qualifications and disciplinary precision.

Quality gates: every deletion must preserve proposition, scope, certainty, and rhetorical function.
