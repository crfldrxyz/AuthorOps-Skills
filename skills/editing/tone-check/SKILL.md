# Tone Check

Infer intended tone from explicit instructions and document context. Flag abrupt shifts, accidental informality, excessive certainty, defensiveness, promotional language, or detached phrasing when they conflict with purpose.

Quality gates: state the inferred target tone and uncertainty rather than imposing a generic style.
