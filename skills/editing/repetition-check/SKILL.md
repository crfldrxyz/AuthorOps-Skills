# Repetition Check

Inspect lexical, syntactic, conceptual, and rhetorical repetition. Determine whether recurrence is intentional, useful, or distracting. Suggest variation only when it improves readability without distorting meaning.

Quality gates: preserve meaningful technical terminology and deliberate motifs.
