# Redundancy Check

Compare nearby and document-level statements for duplicated propositions, repeated definitions, and unnecessary restatement. Preserve deliberate emphasis when it serves a clear rhetorical purpose.

Quality gates: separate repetition from reinforcement and avoid deleting distinctions.
