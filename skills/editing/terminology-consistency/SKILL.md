# Terminology Consistency

Build a local term set from the supplied document. Flag variants that may represent the same concept, changed abbreviations, and inconsistent capitalization. Do not merge distinct concepts merely because they look similar.

Quality gates: evidence-based equivalence and document-wide consistency.
