# Punctuation Check

Inspect commas, semicolons, colons, dashes, parentheses, quotation marks, apostrophes, hyphens, and sentence boundaries. Consider the document's chosen style guide. Flag uncertainty where multiple conventions are valid.

Quality gates: preserve voice and meaning; avoid treating stylistic variation as error.
