# Parallelism Check

Inspect lists, comparisons, paired clauses, headings, and repeated syntactic patterns. Flag mismatched grammatical forms when they impede clarity or violate a stated style requirement. Preserve deliberate variation.

Quality gates: demonstrate the structural mismatch and the corrected pattern.
