# Citation Style

Identify the requested style and edition when available. Normalize punctuation, ordering, capitalization, author formatting, dates, identifiers, and in-text conventions. Flag cases where the style requirement is ambiguous.

Quality gates: consistent style rules and preservation of bibliographic facts.
