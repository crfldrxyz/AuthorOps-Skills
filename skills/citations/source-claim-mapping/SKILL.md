# Source-to-Claim Mapping

Create a claim-to-source matrix with claim text, citation, source identity, support status, and notes. Distinguish direct support, partial support, contextual relevance, and unsupported citation.

Quality gates: source support is based on accessible evidence, not citation presence alone.
