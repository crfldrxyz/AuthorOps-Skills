# Citation Check

Map substantive claims to their citations and references. Check that cited sources are actually relevant to the proposition, that in-text citations resolve to references, and that references are used consistently.

Distinguish formatting errors from evidentiary problems. Do not claim a source supports a proposition unless the source content has been inspected.

Output citation-to-claim findings with severity and evidence.
