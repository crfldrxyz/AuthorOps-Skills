# Manuscript Readiness

Assess major dimensions: structure, coherence, clarity, surface correctness, evidence/citations where applicable, consistency, and unresolved high-severity issues. This is a readiness assessment, not a substitute for specialist review.

Quality gates: report missing evidence/tool access and prioritize material risks.
